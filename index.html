What we are doing

The PAWS Can Drive

A two week push by Team B8 to put food in the bowls at Centre County PAWS, and our team term project for MGMT 215, Entrepreneurial Mindset, at Penn State.

The task. Our class gave every team the same challenge: collect cans of dog food and cat food for Centre County PAWS, the nonprofit animal shelter near campus, without spending a cent of our own money. Donors can hand us real cans and bags, but most of the drive happens online, so we set up this GoFundMe and are spreading it through friends, family, texts and social media.

Where the money goes. The GoFundMe is a charity fundraiser with Centre County PAWS, a registered 501(c)(3), as the beneficiary. Donations go to the shelter directly and are used to buy pet food for the dogs and cats living there.

Who we are. Team B8, a group of Penn State students in MGMT 215, Entrepreneurial Mindset, at the Smeal College of Business, Fall 2026. Brady Murray is the organizer on GoFundMe. Student can drives for PAWS are a tradition in this class: read about past years on Penn State News.

  1. You donate any amount on our GoFundMe, or give us real cans or bags of pet food.
  2. We count every dollar as cans using the class tally sheet, and the shelter turns it into food.
  3. At the end of the drive the whole class adds up its cans and PAWS gets all of it.

Our story

Word for word from our GoFundMe

This is the story our team wrote on the GoFundMe page, exactly as it appears there.

Help fill empty bellies at Centre County PAWS❤️.

Every dog and cat deserves a full belly, a safe place to sleep, and a chance at a loving home and a loving family. Unfortunately many homeless dogs depend on shelters like Centre County PAWS for their daily meals, care, and needs.

Our team is raising money to help provide food for these animals in needs. Every donation no matter how big or small, will help fill an empty belly and make a difference in an animals life.

These animals can’t ask for themselves, so we are asking on their behalf. Please consider donating or sharing our fundraiser to help us reach our challenging goal. Together we can show these animals that they are not forgotten and there is enough love to go around!

Help us turn their empty bowls into full bellies. Every little bit counts!! ❤️

Our product

Adopt. Foster. Support.

The Paws for a Cause tee, designed by Team B8. White shirt, navy print, a small mark on the front and the shelter's two kinds of residents on the back. $20 each in small, medium, large and extra large, and the money goes into the drive like any other donation.

Why these two

A kitten and a pit bull are the animals people pass over most at shelters. Putting them front and centre is the whole message.

Why one colour

Navy on white keeps printing simple and the shirt wearable every day, so the message keeps walking around campus after the drive ends.

How it was made

We ran the class ideation process: ten ideas each on slips of paper, a team vote, our own design against an AI alternate, then a final vote.

Where the money goes

Centre County PAWS

A volunteer run, member supported, non euthanasia shelter that has been finding homes for Centre County's cats and dogs since 1980.

“Promote Animal Welfare and Safety with compassion through adoption, sheltering, education, and community assistance.”

The mission of Centre County PAWS, in their words.

500+cats and dogs find homes through PAWS every year
100+volunteers clean, feed, walk and care for the animals
52 / 120average days a dog, then a cat, waits at the shelter for a home
