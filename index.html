Our story

Word for word from our GoFundMe

This is the story our team wrote on the GoFundMe page, exactly as it appears there.

Help fill empty bellies at Centre County PAWS❤️.

Every dog and cat deserves a full belly, a safe place to sleep, and a chance at a loving home and a loving family. Unfortunately many homeless dogs depend on shelters like Centre County PAWS for their daily meals, care, and needs.

Our team is raising money to help provide food for these animals in needs. Every donation no matter how big or small, will help fill an empty belly and make a difference in an animals life.

These animals can’t ask for themselves, so we are asking on their behalf. Please consider donating or sharing our fundraiser to help us reach our challenging goal. Together we can show these animals that they are not forgotten and there is enough love to go around!

Help us turn their empty bowls into full bellies. Every little bit counts!! ❤️

1401 Trout Road, State College, PA 16801. Visits by appointment.
